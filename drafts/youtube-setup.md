## NOTE ON THE DECISION

The candidate declined a campaign channel in April and is reversing that here.
Reasons on the record: putting campaign work on Queen of Swords AI's channel
invites the reading that the candidate is content marketing for an AI
consultancy, which the funding rules exist to prevent; YouTube is a host rather
than a feed, so it does not carry the maintenance cost the no-social-accounts
rule was written to avoid; and the archive thesis adopted August 25 makes a
durable, citable home for video worth having.

**Condition:** treat it like the website, not like a feed. Nobody owes it a
weekly upload. A dead channel is worse than no channel.
